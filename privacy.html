Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026.

The short version

This site has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The calculators run entirely in your browser: the numbers you type never leave your device.

What we collect

  • Calculators: nothing. All math runs locally in your browser with data files served from this site.
  • Newsletter (optional): if you subscribe, your email address is sent to and stored by our newsletter provider only to send you our emails. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email. We don't sell or share subscriber lists.
  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

YouTube API Services

Cold Hard Data uses YouTube API Services to publish our own videos and to read our own channel's statistics (views, watch time, audience retention) so we can improve our videos. We don't use YouTube API Services to collect, store or share data about viewers of this site.

By watching our videos on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or revoke access granted to applications at Google security settings.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: contact us.
