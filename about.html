About

Money advice, tested.

Cold Hard Data puts famous money advice and the numbers everyone repeats on trial, using decades of real data. Every video ends with a fair verdict (TRUE, MISLEADING or FALSE) and the receipts: a free calculator so you can rerun the test yourself.

How we work

  • Data first. Every number in a video comes from our own analysis of public data (such as Aswath Damodaran's NYU Stern datasets and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' CPI, retrieved from FRED) or from a quoted source with a link. A script can't be published until every number checks out.
  • Real numbers. We show results after inflation, and after fees when they apply.
  • Fair verdicts. We judge claims, never people. We quote the claim exactly, with source and date, and say what part of it is true.
  • Every start date. No cherry-picked periods: we test every window the data allows and show the worst, the median and the best.

Transparency

The analysis, the calculators and the code are our own. The narration is AI-assisted (a synthetic voice reads a script that a human reviews and approves before anything is published). We never use photos of real people, and our narrator never pretends to be a human expert or an advisor.

Education only. Nothing here or in our videos is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and past returns don't predict future returns. If we ever use affiliate links, we'll say so clearly next to them.
